# Pitch Deck Outline
1. Cover — company and one-line value
2. Problem — evidence and audience
3. Solution — product and workflow
4. Market — source-backed sizing and assumptions
5. Product — screenshots / prototype
6. Business model — pricing and unit economics
7. Go-to-market — channels and conversion assumptions
8. Competition — fair comparison
9. Financial plan — assumptions, cash, risks
10. Ask / next steps — specific request
