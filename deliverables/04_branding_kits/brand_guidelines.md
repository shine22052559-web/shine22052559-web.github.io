# Mini Brand Guidelines
## Logo
Keep clear space around the logo; do not stretch or recolor without approval.
## Color
Use primary color for action and accent color sparingly. Check contrast.
## Typography
Use display type for headings and readable body type for long copy.
## Imagery
Use licensed, relevant images. Avoid misleading mockups.
## Deliverables
List source files, exports, license notes, and version in the handoff.
